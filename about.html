About Our Club

Founded in 2018, we are a vibrant community of young professionals dedicated to social impact.

Our Story

What started as a small group of passionate individuals has grown into one of the most active Rotaract clubs in the region. We believe that young people have the power to drive real change.

Over the years, we've completed more than 200+ projects, touched thousands of lives, and built a community of leaders who are committed to making a difference.
